Fine grain over everything.

One static grain layer and one vignette sit over the canvas and the photos alike, so the 3D world and the club's photos read as one film stock. Both are CSS, inside the tier 1 budget from #5.

The grain never animates: an animated layer repaints the full screen every frame for no story.

Tiles show the grain at three times its strength so it survives a screenshot; the page itself runs the 7% layer.

Decision. Static SVG noise at 7% in overlay blend, plus the radial vignette. Photos are single-tone blue over the night, as #4's strip.

No menu. The rail is the map.

A's waypoint rail is the only navigation and the scroll progress in one: a hairline fills with light as you travel, each waypoint lights as you pass it, the current one glows. Decision. No top nav, no hamburger, no separate progress bar. Labels show on hover and for the current waypoint on desktop; phones show dots only. Waypoint names wait for #9.

Your cursor stays yours. Silence by default.

The pointer is the system's own. Your point is the one thing, and a second dot chasing the mouse would compete with it. Instead the field leans gently toward the pointer on desktop; on phones it leans toward the touch while it lasts.

No sound and no toggle. Most visitors are in Instagram's browser with the sound off, and a toggle is one more control in the corner for nobody.

Decision. Native cursor, field lean as a bonus. Open question 21 keeps its default: no sound.
