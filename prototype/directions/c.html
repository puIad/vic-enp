Vision & Innovation Club

ENP, Algiers. Since 2014.

Chapter 01

The club.

A nonprofit, English-speaking scientific club at ENP, founded in 2014 by a group of ENP students.

VIC gives polytechnic students a place to gain real experience in leading and managing, to be creative and to contribute, and to learn what the curriculum doesn't cover.

The mission: help members, and ENP students in general, develop their soft and hard skills and build their network through events and activities that are genuinely enjoyable. The vision: a lasting community its members are proud to belong to.

Through the door
Chapter 02

Two homes.

Your work side is one of four main departments. Your learning side is one of three activity departments.

HR

The heart of the club's internal life. Keeps the community organized, connected and motivated.

FLER

VIC's link to the outside world: companies, sponsors, guests and suppliers, plus logistics and budget.

IT

Design, visual identity, photography and video. No coding required.

COMM

The bridge between the club and its audience: strategy, captions, social media and partnerships.

Into club life
Chapter 03

Club life.

The Local is VIC's headquarters inside ENP. Every VICer is welcome, without exception.

VIC Entertainment runs at least two community activities a month, built from members' own ideas. Any motivated member can join the team or pitch an activity.

At general assemblies the President keeps everyone updated on projects and progress. Graduates stay in the community as alumni and help with events.

To the horizon

Once a VICer, always a VICer.

Every ENP student can join, in any year and any specialty. On the form, pick your first and second choice among the four main departments. Applications open on 25 October; when they do, apply here and your point joins the network of 600.
