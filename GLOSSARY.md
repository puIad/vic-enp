# Glossary

Terms for the VIC-ENP landing page, settled on the wayfinder map (2026-10-19). The spec is in
[docs/experience.md](docs/experience.md).

- **Act**: one stretch of the journey with one feeling and one device. The page has seven.
- **Direction**: one whole-page option, made of a 3D world, a grammar, colour on the night, a type treatment, a
  motion feel, a hand-over and anchor references. Three were comped (A, B, C); the page is their merge.
- **Your point**: the visitor's own point of light, which travels down the page with them and lands in the Apply
  pill at the close.
- **The 600**: the field of points, one per member.
- **Formation**: a shape the points hold for an act or a cut (the icon, the cloud, a department's shape).
- **Icon formation**: the 600 drawing the VIC icon (bulb and iris) at the start of the hero, your point lit in its
  iris. Not "the dot logo" or "the mark": the points never draw VIC's wordmark.
- **Iris slot**: the close's ring of the 600 around the empty slot in the Apply pill, the one echo of the icon
  formation.
- **Logo formation**: the points drawing a department's or an event's own logo in its cut. It draws the logo's
  symbol, or the name where the name is the logo (GALA, Ignite, POLYMAZE). Not the icon formation, which is VIC's
  own. Avoid: "dot logo".
- **Word scrub**: words lighting up one by one as the visitor scrolls (act 2's title and sentences, the closing
  motto). Avoid: "kinetic text", "text animation".
- **Speed**: how the world moves in an act. A's acts morph slowly; B's acts snap per cut.
- **Cut sequence**: a B act, a run of short cuts where the same points snap into a new formation per cut
  (departments, events).
- **Passage**: one of C's three physical hand-overs (door, tunnel, horizon) between A's speed and B's.
- **Hand-over**: how one act gives way to the next (a morph, a passage or a hard cut).
- **The light act**: act 6 (how to join and the FAQ), the only act on Bright Gray.
- **Waypoint rail**: the fixed column of dots at the left edge, one per act, the page's only navigation. At the
  end it lands in the footer as "Jump back to".
- **Sub-dot**: a smaller rail dot for one cut or block inside an act (a department, an event, Steps, Growth, FAQ).
  The current act's show as ticks; all show with names on hover or a hold.
- **Catch**: the scroll gliding to rest on a catch point: inside a run, every gesture steps one cut; outside, momentum
  that would carry the visitor past a still point stops on it. A drag is never caught in the flow. Avoid: "snap" (the
  formations snap per cut), "scroll lock".
- **Catch point**: one of the 26 places a catch can land: each cut, the peak's resolved photo and its ten photos,
  the top of act 2, the top of the light act, the close at rest.
- **Tier**: the device class chosen at load (0 no canvas, 1 phones, 2 desktops).
- **Comp**: a throwaway HTML and three.js mock of an act on a never-merged `prototype/*` branch.
