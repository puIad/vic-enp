What you get.

Lead early

  • Real responsibility, early

    Organize large events with corporate guests, sponsors and hundreds of attendees.

  • Organizing-team experience

    Work on the club's projects and events.

  • A path to leadership

    Active members can become Project Managers, Department Heads, or run for President.

  • A voice

    Propose ideas to the committee and vote in elections.

Grow your skills

  • Free internal trainings

    Technical and soft skills.

  • Two homes in the club

    One main department for your work, one activity department for your learning.

  • Certificates

    A membership certificate, plus event certificates for organizing teams.

Belong for good

  • A network

    Sponsors, industry leaders, alumni and members from every ENP specialty.

  • The Local

    The club's headquarters at ENP. Every VICer is welcome, without exception.

  • A lifelong community

    Graduates stay in VIC as alumni.
