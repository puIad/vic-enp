How to join.

Every ENP student can apply, in any year and any specialty. Applications open 25 October 2026.

  3. 01

    Fill in the form.

    Pick your first and second choice among the four main departments: HR, FLER, IT and COMM.

  4. 02

    Check your inbox.

    You get a confirmation email with your registration code.

  5. 03

    Set up your interview.

    If your application is accepted, two club members contact you within a day to set up your interview. You choose your activity department there: SPARK, Training or English Bubble.

  6. 04

    Join your teams.

    Get your result and start with your departments.

Then grow.

Joining is the start. Members move on in many directions.

  1. Organising teamsJoin the organising team of an event.
