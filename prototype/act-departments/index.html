Act 2 stand-inWhat VIC is, in numbers.

Seven departments. You pick one main and one activity.

HR · main 1 of 4

Run the community.

HR is the heart of the club's internal life. You keep members connected and motivated, and you speak for the club to the school administration.

People managementOrganizationReportingEvent operations

Suits organized people who care about the community.

What you'd do in HR

  • Recruit and welcome new members
  • Follow up on members' engagement and motivation
  • Run VIC Entertainment, the fun side of the club
  • Organize logistics on event day

FLER · main 2 of 4

Win the sponsors.

FLER is the club's link to companies, associations and guests, plus its logistics and budget.

NegotiationSponsorshipBudgetingLogistics

Suits confident communicators who want hands-on experience with the professional world.

What you'd do in FLER

  • Contact companies and secure sponsorships for events
  • Build relationships with partners, institutions and alumni
  • Manage venues, sound, screens, catering and printables
  • Look for TV, radio and press appearances

IT · main 3 of 4

Design the club.

IT at VIC means design and media, not programming. You make every poster, post, banner, video and photo.

Design and media, not codingBrandingPhotographyVideography

Suits creatives, designers, photographers and anyone who wants to learn.

What you'd do in IT

  • Design posts, stories, posters and flyers
  • Build a full visual identity for each event
  • Join the Photography and Videography team
  • Join FARI9 TI9NI, the tech crew on event day

COMM · main 4 of 4

Tell the story.

COMM is the bridge between the club and its audience: its public image, its events and its reach.

CopywritingContent strategySocial mediaStorytelling

Suits writers, storytellers and natural communicators.

What you'd do in COMM

  • Plan the communication for each event
  • Write captions, slogans, scripts and emails
  • Run VIC's social media
  • Build media partnerships and join the TikTok Content Team

SPARK · activity 1 of 3

Build robots.

SPARK is science, tech and robotics: hands-on workshops, competitions and a free learning platform.

WorkshopsCompetitionsFree learning platform

What you'd do in SPARK

  • Join workshops chosen from members' needs
  • Compete under SPARK's name, with technical support
  • Work on internal projects and research
  • Learn from free courses that fill curriculum gaps

Training · activity 2 of 3

Choose your skills.

At the start of the year you pick the skills you want to learn. The yearly plan is built around your answers.

Soft skillsTechnical skillsPeer-to-peer sessions

What you'd do in Training

  • Attend sessions led by internal and external trainers
  • Teach what you are good at, peer to peer
  • Join learning groups with practical tasks and progress tracking

English Bubble · activity 3 of 3

Speak with confidence.

English Bubble is a relaxed place to practice English: debates, games, book discussions and social nights.

DebatesGamesBook discussionsSocial nights

What you'd do in English Bubble

  • Talk and share ideas in a relaxed setting
  • Build confidence, public speaking and leadership
  • Try new things in a safe space
