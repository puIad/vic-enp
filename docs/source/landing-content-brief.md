## 2. About VIC

VIC was founded in 2014 to give polytechnic students a freeing environment to gain real experience in **leading and managing**, unleash their **creativity and sense of contribution**, and complement their studies with trainings that fill in what the curriculum doesn't cover. ✅

### Mission ✅
Help members — and ENP students in general — **develop their soft and hard skills** and **build their network** through events and activities that are genuinely enjoyable.

### Vision ✅
Create an enriching experience for members and a **lasting community** they're proud to belong to.

### What VIC aims to do (public "About") 🌐
1. Complement the training of future engineers with both hard skills and soft skills.
2. Offer a flexible environment where students put theory into practice.
3. Inspire — and be inspired — through a wide diversity of events.
4. Build leadership and management experience by working with people from different backgrounds.
5. Take concrete action as a team and grow your skill set.

### Core values ✅
- **Respect** — the club's most essential shared value.
- **Diversity** — respect for cultural, religious and political differences inside the community.
- **Contribution & creativity** — members propose ideas and shape the club.
- **Legacy** — every generation documents and mentors the next.

## 3. Why Join — What You Get ✅

- **Real responsibility early**: organize large-scale events with corporate guests, sponsors and hundreds of attendees.
- **Two homes in the club**: join **one main department** (your work side) and **one activity department** (your learning side).
- **Free internal trainings** in technical and soft skills.
- **Organizing-team experience** on the club's projects and events.
- **A path to leadership**: active members can become Project Managers, Department Heads, or run for President.
- **Certificates**: a membership certificate, plus event certificates for organizing teams.
- **A voice**: propose ideas to the committee and vote in elections.
- **Network**: sponsors, industry leaders, alumni and members from every ENP specialty.
- **A space of your own**: "The Local," the club's headquarters at ENP — every VICer is welcome, without exception.
- **Lifelong community**: graduates stay in the VIC community as alumni.

## 4. The Four Main Departments

> Candidates pick **one**. Each card on the page could show: what the department does → what you'd do → skills you'll build → who it suits.

### 4.1 HR — Human Resources ✅
**What it does:** The heart of the club's internal life. Keeps the community organized, connected and motivated, and handles the club's relationship with the school administration.

**What you'd do:**
- Recruit and welcome new members.
- Follow up on members' engagement and motivation.
- Attend department meetings and write reports.
- Organize logistics on event day (D-Day).
- Run **VIC Entertainment** — the fun side of the club (see §6).
- Prepare official requests to the ENP administration.

**Skills you'll build:** people management, organization, reporting, conflict resolution, administrative writing (French), event operations.

**Suits:** organized people who care about the community and the people in it.

---

### 4.2 FLER — Finance, Logistics & External Relations ✅
**What it does:** VIC's link to the outside world — companies, associations, guests, suppliers, media — plus the club's logistics and budget.

**What you'd do:**
- Contact companies and secure **sponsorships** for events.
- Build relationships with partners, institutions, training centers and alumni.
- Manage event logistics: venues, sound, screens, catering, goodies and printables.
- Look for media appearances (TV, radio, press) for the club.
- Help plan budgets and keep the club's contact and supplier databases up to date.

**Skills you'll build:** negotiation, business communication, leadership, sponsorship, budgeting, logistics, staying calm under pressure, presenting yourself to companies.

**Suits:** confident communicators who want hands-on experience with the professional and industrial world.

> ⚠️ **Eligibility note:** you can't be in FLER if you're in the external-relations team of another club or student branch, or on another club's committee (being a member of a technical team like Eurobot, Formula Student or Rocketry is fine).

---

### 4.3 IT — Design, Visual Identity & Media ✅
**What it does:** Creates the club's visual identity — every poster, post, banner, badge, certificate, video and photo — and gives technical and artistic support to communication during events.

**What you'd do:**
- Design social-media posts, stories, reel thumbnails, posters and flyers.
- Design printables: banners, photowalls, badges, certificates, brochures, stickers, T-shirts.
- Build a full **visual identity (event charter)** for each event.
- Join the **Photography & Videography (P&V) team** to cover events, shoot and edit.
- Join **"FARI9 TI9NI"**, the D-Day tech crew that captures, edits and publishes content live on event day.

**Skills you'll build:** graphic design, branding, typography, photography, videography, photo/video editing, working to deadlines.

**Tools and gear you'll touch:** Photoshop, Illustrator, Lightroom / Capture One, Premiere Pro / DaVinci Resolve; club gear includes Canon 6D and 80D bodies, Canon 24–105mm f/4L and 50mm f/1.4 lenses, Hollyland Lark M1 mics, tripods and a DJI Ronin-S gimbal.

**P&V trainings:** photography (cameras, RAW vs JPG, exposure triangle, lenses, composition), videography (frame rates, motion blur, shots and camera movement, gimbal, lighting) and photo editing.

**Suits:** creatives, designers, photographers and anyone who wants to learn.

> Note: the IT department here means **design and media**, not programming. Worth saying clearly on the page so CS students aren't misled. ⚠️ Confirm how you want to phrase this.

---

### 4.4 COMM — Communication ✅
**What it does:** The bridge between the club and its audience. Manages VIC's public image, promotes its events, and grows its reach.

**What you'd do:**
- Plan the communication strategy for each event (what to post, when, where).
- Write captions, slogans, scripts, emails to participants, and documents for FLER (sponsorship files, presentations).
- Run VIC's social media: posting, replying to messages, moderating comments.
- Build **media partnerships** with other clubs and pages.
- Film and post live stories on event day.
- Join the **TikTok Content Team** — trends, short videos, the good vibes of every event.

**Platforms you'll work on:** Instagram & Facebook (main reach), LinkedIn (big announcements, sponsors, recaps), YouTube (long-form, mini-podcasts, recaps), TikTok (short-form, trends).

**Skills you'll build:** copywriting, content strategy, social-media management, marketing, storytelling, partnership management.

**Suits:** writers, storytellers, trend-watchers and natural communicators.

## 5. The Three Activity Departments (Learning Side)

> Candidates also pick **one**. These are where members learn and practice. Every activity department runs at least one activity per month. ✅

### 5.1 SPARK — Science, Tech & Robotics ✅
- For members interested in science and technology, **with a main focus on robotics**.
- Hands-on **workshops** throughout the year, chosen based on members' needs and upcoming competitions.
- **Competitions:** SPARK enters competitions and welcomes teams to compete under its name, with technical support (and financial support when possible).
- **Internal projects**, practical tasks and research activities.
- **Online sessions** where members research and present technical topics.
- A **free learning platform** with courses designed to fill gaps in the academic curriculum, created as a *Waqf Lillah* (charitable) project.
- Its own **Instagram page** for projects, achievements and opportunities.
- Brand color: orange → yellow lightning logo.

### 5.2 Training — Skills for Professionals ✅
- Soft skills, technical skills and professional expertise through training sessions and workshops.
- Both **internal and external** trainers, plus **peer-to-peer sessions** where members teach what they're good at.
- At the start of the year, members **choose the skills they want to learn** through a form — the yearly plan is built around their answers.
- Multi-session trainings run as **learning groups** with practical tasks and progress tracking.
- Recent sessions 🌐: Public Speaking, Engineering & AI Tools, Mastering Linux, and masterclasses in Figma (UI/UX), Adobe Lightroom and Algerian Pop Art.

### 5.3 English Bubble (EB) — Speak with Confidence ✅
- Practice English by talking and sharing ideas in a relaxed setting.
- **Debates, games, book discussions and social nights.**
- Builds confidence, public speaking and leadership.
- A safe space to try new things and express yourself.
- Its own **Instagram page**.

## 7. Flagship Events & Projects

> All events are organized **by the whole club** — every department plays a part. ✅ The first five names below are VIC's original events and are fixed in the club's charter. ✅

### Engineers' Gala ✅🌐
- VIC's prestige event, with a fixed **gold, prestigious** visual identity.
- Brings **national and international corporate leaders and researchers together with newly graduated engineers** to introduce them to the professional world, expand their network and inspire future industry leaders. 🌐
- Format includes guest talks and roundtables with CEOs, entrepreneurs and researchers. 🌐
- History 🌐: 3rd edition in 2019 · 4th edition in July 2021 at the Marriott Hotel · 5th edition on 17 July 2022 at the Sheraton Club des Pins · an edition in autumn 2024 at the Marriott.
- **9th edition coming this year (2026).** ✅ Teasers and announcements are posted on Instagram **@vic.enp** — link there from the page.

### STEPS — Students, Tests, Exchange Programs & Scholarships ✅🌐
- Everything about studying abroad, language tests and scholarships.
- 2026 🌐: workshops (e.g., DELF / DALF / TCF prep) and the **"STEPS Abroad"** YouTube series (Episode 1: France — Campus France, visas, housing; Episode 2: Italy — scholarships, expenses, housing, visas).

### AEC — Algerian Engineering Competition ✅🌐
- National engineering competition.
- First edition (Dec 2018) 🌐 was organized with the City of Algiers and student clubs across Algeria and the diaspora, as a **decentralized competition in 6 Algerian cities** (Algiers, Oran, Tlemcen, Constantine, Annaba, Ouargla), themed around leapfrog technologies and industrial innovation.
- **Latest edition: 2026.** ✅

### Anglosphere ✅
- A **linguistic event celebrating the beauty and diversity of the English language**.
- Brings together **conferences, workshops and stands** built around that theme.

### VICra ✅
- VIC's **Ramadan project**.
- Throughout Ramadan, the VIC page publishes a series of **Islamic reminders (mawa'idh)**, each episode teaching an important lesson for Muslims.
- Every episode is followed by a **quiz open to everyone**, with prizes such as **books and qamis**.

### Ignite Algiers ✅🌐
- An international event format with a fixed **red** identity.
- Short, powerful talks from industry leaders, innovators and thought leaders. 🌐
- 4th edition in 2024; **5th edition** announced November 2025. 🌐

### Hour of Code ✅
- International event with a fixed **blue** identity.
- Latest edition: **2023**. ✅ (Present it as part of VIC's history rather than an upcoming event.)

### Polymaze 🌐
- VIC's **robotics competition** (2026): line-follower and maze-solving robots.
- Preparation workshops for registered participants (e.g., *Intro to Maze-Solving Robots*), then a competition day at ENP.
- Great visual for a 3D hero (a robot navigating a maze).

### CPSP — Cycle Préparatoire Starter Pack ✅🌐
- A project to **guide ENP newcomers**: discover their new school and get practical tips for succeeding in the **Prepa Cycle**.
- Features guests who were **class majors** (top students) in different classes.
- 2026 edition held in September. 🌐
- Especially relevant on a recruitment page aimed at first-years — a good "VIC already looked out for you" hook.

## 8. How to Join

### Who can join ✅
- **Every ENP student** — any year, any specialty.

### When ✅
- **2026 recruitment opens on 20 October 2026.**
- Registrations open **mainly at the start of the academic year**.
- A **sponsorship ("Parrainage") session** may open once or twice a year, where members can invite friends to join.
- ⚠️ Closing date: not set yet — leave a placeholder or omit (a countdown to the opening works well until 20 Oct).

### What you choose ✅
1. **One main department** — HR, FLER, IT or COMM.
2. **One activity department** — SPARK, Training or English Bubble.

### Process
1. **Fill in the registration form** — it lives on its own page of the same website (e.g., `/join`), linked from every CTA on the landing page.
2. **Application review** — if your application is accepted, you're invited to an interview.
3. **Interview within 48 hours** of registering, with two club members, based on your first-choice department.
4. Get your result and join your teams.

### Ways to grow after joining ✅
- Join an event's **organizing team**.
- Help across departments once you gain experience.
- Apply to be a **Project Manager** or take on a responsibility role.
- Join specialized teams: **P&V**, **TikTok Content Team**, **VIC Entertainment**, **FARI9 TI9NI** (D-Day crew).
- Run in **elections** for Head of Department or President.
- Join the **Research & Development Squad**, which reviews and improves the club's charters (open to members with experience; a few active new members may join as an exception).

### What VIC asks of you (keep light on the page) ✅
- Respect everyone in the community.
- Take part in events and activities.
- Meet the deadlines of the tasks you take on.
- Represent the club proudly, at ENP and online.

## 9. FAQ (Ready to Use)

**Do I need experience?**
No. Trainings and workshops are part of membership, and VIC's whole purpose is to help you learn. (For P&V specifically, interest is enough, though owning a camera is a plus.)

**I'm in first year — can I join?**
Yes. Any ENP student can join.

**Is the club only in English?**
English is the club's official language. English Bubble exists precisely to help you get comfortable speaking it.

**Can I join more than one department?**
At the start, one main department + one activity department. Once you have experience, you can help with tasks across departments.

**I'm in another club. Can I still join?**
Yes, with one exception: if you're in another club's external-relations team, you can't join FLER (and vice versa). Committee members of other clubs also can't be on VIC's committee.

**Is the IT department about coding?**
No. IT at VIC means design, visual identity, photography and video.

**Do I get a certificate?**
Yes. Active members can request a membership certificate, and organizing-team members receive event certificates.

**When does recruitment open, and how fast is the process?**
Registration opens on 20 October 2026. If your application is accepted, you'll be interviewed within 48 hours of registering.

**What happens after I graduate?**
You become an alumnus and stay part of the VIC community. *Once a VICer, always a VICer.*

## 12. Brand Kit for the Build ✅

### Core colors
| Name | HEX | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Cyan Cornflower Blue | `#127DBB` | Primary (logo gradient start) |
| Medium Aquamarine | `#69C4AF` | Primary (logo gradient end) |
| Dark Jungle Green | `#141E27` | Dark backgrounds / text |
| Bright Gray | `#EFEFEF` | Light backgrounds |

Rules: use **only black or white text** on core colors (approved for accessibility). Logo gradient is **blue → green**.

### Activity department colors
| Department | Colors |
|---|---|
| SPARK | Orange `#F29A09` → Tangelo `#F24407` (orange-to-yellow gradient logo) |
| Training | Cyan Cobalt Blue `#1A52A2` (darker blue gradient) |
| English Bubble | Classic VIC gradient (`#127DBB` → `#69C4AF`) |
| Also listed | Dark Violet `#5700CF`, Medium Purple `#7849A8` |

### Typography
| Font | Use |
|---|---|
| **Gilroy** | All VIC typography. Headings: Bold / ExtraBold. Body: Regular / Medium. Max 3 weights per design. No all-caps or all-lowercase. |
| Gotham | Logo only (Bold "VISION & INNOVATION", Light "CLUB"); allowed on some SPARK designs |
| Madani | Arabic text (set ~15% larger than Latin) |
| Ubuntu Arabic | Alternative for non-Latin script |
| Bahnschrift | English Bubble designs |

> ⚠️ Gilroy and Gotham are commercial fonts — check web licensing or pick close web-safe alternatives for the page.

### Logo rules relevant to a 3D page
- Icon = a **light bulb** (Innovation, "I") merged with an **iris** (Vision, "V").
- Horizontal logo is primary; stacked logo for large scale only.
- **Always use the provided files — never recreate the logo**, and the bulb and iris shapes **must not be extracted** as separate graphic elements.
- No cropping, opacity changes, recoloring, distortion, outlines, drop shadows/effects, rotating parts, or changing the typeface.
- Full-color logo only on white or black backgrounds (or a black/white area of a photo).

> ⚠️ This means a 3D hero **can't build a 3D version of the logo or animate its bulb/iris parts**. The 3D concept should be original (e.g., abstract light/glass forms, a maze, a network of nodes) with the flat logo placed per the rules — or get explicit approval for an exception.

### Media specs (for hero posters/videos)
- Video: 1080p at 25–30 fps; watermark logo top-left at 50% opacity.
- Event photos: framed, 2500×2000px.
