## What matters most, in the order it would bite

1. **A 2026 applicant cannot register again if the 2027 Master still lists them.** The Redis mark `reg:<sha256(email)>` has no expiry (`api/_lib/mark.ts:1`, `api/_lib/submit.ts:101`). Before refusing an address, `/api/code` and `/api/submit` check it against the Master's `REG_TAB` and `IV_TAB` (`api/_lib/mark.ts`, `api/_lib/sheets.ts:addressListed`). A mark whose address is in neither tab is dropped. So a fresh 2027 Master with fresh tabs frees everyone; reusing the 2026 Master and tabs keeps every 2026 applicant locked out with the "registered" answer. (code)
2. **The registration questions are the Master's column headers.** `src/apply/questions.ts:1-4` says the 32 titles match the Master's `Registration Responses` headers character for character (odd spaces included), and `src/apply/questions.test.ts` holds them equal to `docs/source/registration-questions.md`. `api/_lib/health.ts` reports red when a header is missing. Changing one question title means changing the site, the doc copy, the Master header and the script's parser together (`R:Registrations.gs:3`, `regHeaders_`). `R:docs/registration-form.md` ("After the season") says the same. (code)
3. **Four mail templates carry "2026" in three places each**, and a Master Settings row can override each one (see section 4). Changing the default text in `R:Config.gs` does not change a mail whose `MAIL_<ID>_<FIELD>` Settings row is filled. (code)
4. **The bound Apps Script is tied to one Master by id** (`MASTER_ID` script property, `CONFIG` ids in `R:Config.gs:30-35`), and its two Google Forms, its binome folder and its 2025 team database are 2026 files. A new season is a copy of the Master plus new ids, not a date change. (code)
